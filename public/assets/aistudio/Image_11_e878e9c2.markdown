## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intellectual rigor, classical dignity, and creative vitality of a premier University Department of English Language & Literature and its Student Activities Unit. Inspired by storied publications like *The Atlantic*, *The New Yorker*, and *Harvard Gazette*, the interface treats digital screens as fine printed broadsheets—measured, authoritative, and deliberate.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Editorial Broadsheet** with **Subtle Scholarly Classicism**. Rather than relying on ephemeral UI trends, the aesthetic favors typographic distinction, hairline rules, disciplined structural grids, and warm, paper-inspired tactile tones. The user experience should evoke the contemplative feeling of browsing a collegiate archival quarterly: clear hierarchies, immaculate typographic pacing, and zero visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intellectual, grounded atmosphere anchored by scholarly tradition and warm parchment tones:

- **Primary (`#1A365D`)**: Deep Navy Blue. Serves as the principal branding anchor, applied to mastheads, key navigational headers, primary buttons, major titles, and authoritative framing elements.
- **Secondary (`#D69E2E`)**: Warm Amber Gold. Deployed selectively as an intellectual illumination color—curated badges, award laurels, section kickers, pulled quotes, and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#2B6CB0`)**: Rich Royal Blue. Used for contextual navigation, secondary actions, metadata links, inline hyperlinks, and subtle visual contrast against deep navy.
- **Neutral (`#1A202C`)**: Deep Charcoal. Delivers high-contrast, comfortable long-form legibility without the harshness of pure black.

### Surface Hierarchy & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base**: `#FBFBFA` (Warm Parchment/Alabaster) provides an optical softness reminiscent of archival rag paper.
- **Surface Elevation / Cards**: `#FFFFFF` provides clean separation for critical articles, essays, and featured student profiles.
- **Surface Muted / Inset**: `#F3F4F6` provides subtle contrast for aside notes, footnotes, syllabus excerpts, and quote cards.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Low-contrast warm rules (`#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`) echo literary column rules and typographic em-dashes.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs the stately cadence of **Playfair Display** for editorial headlines and mastheads with the contemporary clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body texts, metadata, and interface controls.

- **Editorial Headings**: Utilize Playfair Display with generous line heights to prevent descender collisions. Use italic weights deliberately for article subheadings, literary quotes, and author commentary.
- **Long-Form Reading**: Set in Plus Jakarta Sans at 16px to 18px with relaxed leading (minimum 1.6x) to facilitate scholarly comprehension without eye fatigue.
- **Section Kickers & Badges**: Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with uppercase transformation, bold weight, and increased tracking (`0.12em`) to mirror traditional academic rubrics and departmental identifiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The magazine operates on an editorial grid inspired by classical newspaper multi-column layouts, paired with a modern responsive framework:

- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max Width 1280px)**: Primary canvas utilizes a 12-column layout. Editorial features commonly span an asymmetrical 8-column reading well flanked by a 4-column contextual sidebar (citations, upcoming literary symposiums, student spotlight notes).
- **Tablet (8 Columns, Max Width 1024px)**: Transition into a single dominant reading track with inline sidebars, 2-column departmental activity summaries, and 2.5rem outer canvas padding.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Single unified column. Layout items stack linearly, margins tighten to 1.25rem, and vertical rhythm relies on subtle horizontal rules rather than heavy boxed spacing.

Editorial pacing relies on rhythmic vertical whitespace (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between narrative sections, ensuring each literary submission or departmental bulletin breathes naturally.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve academic authenticity, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows and artificial skeuomorphism. Depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering** and **Low-Contrast Architectural Hairlines**:

- **Paper Layers (Elevation Zero & One)**: The baseline canvas sits on warm paper (`#FBFBFA`). Elevated cards and essay features use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) bound by crisp 1px borders in `#E2E8F0` or `#CBD5E1`.
- **Delicate Ambient Shadows**: For interactive popovers, dropdown archives, and active navigational modals, depth is signaled via an ultra-subtle, warm-diffused veil: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.03)`.
- **Editorial Separators**: Content depth between columns and feature grids is defined by classic hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), with featured editorial dividers receiving a double-line accent or small centered amber diamond (`#D69E2E`).

## Shapes

The shape architecture is calibrated to **Soft (Level 1)**, reflecting bookbinding and archival letterpress structures. Elements remain disciplined, crisp, and predominantly rectilinear:

- **Containers & Editorial Cards**: Strict 0.25rem (`rounded`) or flat 0px corners, maintaining the structured discipline of broadsheets.
- **Interactive Badges & Meta Tags**: Subtle 0.25rem corner curves that present a tailored, stamp-like appearance.
- **Inputs & Call-to-Actions**: Standardized at 0.25rem for an intentional, scholarly feel—neither starkly brutalist nor overly bubbly.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Navy Blue (`#1A365D`) fill with crisp white text, 0.25rem border-radius, and slight padding (`0.75rem 1.5rem`). Hover state shifts to Rich Royal Blue (`#2B6CB0`) with a smooth 150ms transition.
- **Secondary / Academic Outline**: Transparent background with a 1px border in `#1A365D` and primary navy text. Hover introduces a delicate tint of `#1A365D` at 5% opacity.
- **Accent**: Warm Amber Gold (`#D69E2E`) background with `#1A202C` text for callout submissions, literary prizes, or urgent student deadlines.

### Editorial Cards
- **Featured Cover Story**: Clean white background framed by a hairline border (`#E2E8F0`). Incorporates an uppercase category kicker (`label-kicker` in `#D69E2E`), a large Playfair Display headline, an excerpt in Plus Jakarta Sans, and an author byline with reading time.
- **Compact Bulletin / Student Gazette Item**: Minimalist card using horizontal orientation on desktop (thumbnail on left, metadata and title on right) separated by a 1px bottom divider.

### Chips & Badges
- **Departmental Tags**: Compact uppercase elements styled with `label-kicker`. Background is a pale warm wash (`#FEFCF6`), bordered by Warm Amber Gold (`#D69E2E` at 40% opacity) with text in `#1A365D`.
- **Genre & Activity Badges**: Soft neutral background (`#F1F5F9`) with `#1A202C` text for tags such as *Critical Essay*, *Poetry*, *Department News*, and *Faculty Research*.

### Form Elements & Text Inputs
- **Inputs & Textareas**: Set with a warm white base, framed by a 1px `#CBD5E1` border. Focused state replaces the default ring with a sharp 1.5px border in `#1A365D` and an amber gold glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(214, 158, 46, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Square or round forms with 1px border in `#1A365D`. Checked state fills with `#1A365D` and displays a crisp white check or center pip.

### Literary Lists & Curated Indexes
- **Archive Row**: Numbered or chronologically labeled rows divided by fine 1px rules. The index number is displayed in italicized Playfair Display (`#D69E2E`), followed by the article title in bold navy and author attribution in neutral gray.

### Pull Quotes & Critical Excerpts
- Centered or left-aligned pull quotes set in Playfair Display italic (`24px`). Flanked by a vertical 3px left border in Warm Amber Gold (`#D69E2E`), or framed top and bottom by twin classical hairlines.
